## Permission justifications

### whatsapp_business_messaging

> Monotron is a multi-tenant customer support inbox. After a workspace administrator
> connects their WhatsApp Business Account through Facebook Login, Monotron receives
> customer WhatsApp messages through the Cloud API webhook and sends replies from the
> connected business phone number. Replies are generated by the workspace's AI using its
> own knowledge base, or written by a human agent in the shared inbox. The permission is
> used only for the WhatsApp account the administrator explicitly connected, and each
> workspace can only access its own conversations.

### whatsapp_business_management

> Monotron uses this permission during the connection flow to read the WhatsApp Business
> Account and phone number selected by the administrator, and to subscribe the connected
> account to the `messages` webhook. It is not used to change business settings, create
> templates, or access accounts the administrator did not connect. Disconnecting the
> channel removes the stored connection and stops new messages from being imported.

### business_management

> Monotron uses this permission only during the Facebook Login flow so the administrator
> can select the business portfolio that owns the WhatsApp Business Account being
> connected. The selected business id is stored with the connection for diagnostics and
> routing. Monotron does not modify business settings or access other businesses.

### instagram_basic

> Monotron reads the Instagram professional account id and username after the
> administrator completes Instagram API with Unified Login. The username is shown on the
> Channels page so the team can confirm which account is connected. No Instagram content
> is read beyond the account identity needed for messaging.

### instagram_manage_messages

> Monotron receives Instagram direct messages for the connected professional account
> through Meta webhooks and sends replies from the shared inbox. Replies are either
> AI-generated from the workspace knowledge base or written by a human agent. The
> permission is used only for the Instagram account the administrator explicitly
> connected, and conversations are isolated per workspace.

## Screencast script (4 minutes)

Record the full flow in one continuous video with narration. Show the browser URL and
the phone screen when relevant. Do not show tokens, secrets, or the `.env` file.

### 0:00–0:20 — Introduction

Show:

- `https://www.monotron.in` and the Monotron workspace.
- The Channels page with Gmail, WhatsApp, and Instagram cards.

Say:

> Monotron is a multi-tenant AI customer support inbox. A workspace administrator
> connects the business's own channels, and the team answers customers from one inbox.

### 0:20–1:00 — Connect WhatsApp

Show:

1. Click **Connect WhatsApp**.
2. Facebook Login opens.
3. Log in with the business administrator account.
4. Select the business portfolio and the WhatsApp Business Account.
5. Grant `whatsapp_business_management`, `whatsapp_business_messaging`, and
   `business_management`.
6. Return to Monotron and show the connected phone number.

Say:

> The administrator explicitly chooses the business and phone number. Monotron stores
> the connection for this workspace only and subscribes the account to message
> webhooks.

### 1:00–1:40 — Receive a customer message

Show:

1. On a customer phone, send `Hi, what are your support hours?` to the business number.
2. Open **Inbox** in Monotron.
3. Show the new WhatsApp conversation and the customer message.

Say:

> Meta delivers the customer message through the `messages` webhook. Monotron resolves
> the workspace from the connected phone number id and creates a conversation.

### 1:40–2:20 — AI reply and human reply

Show:

1. With **AI auto-reply** on, show the AI answer arriving on the customer phone.
2. In Monotron, open the same conversation and send a human reply.
3. Show the human reply arriving on the customer phone.

Say:

> The AI answers from the workspace knowledge base. A human agent can take over and
> reply from the same inbox; the reply is sent through the WhatsApp Cloud API.

### 2:20–3:00 — Connect Instagram

Show:

1. Click **Connect Instagram**.
2. Complete Unified Login.
3. Select the Instagram professional account.
4. Grant `instagram_basic` and `instagram_manage_messages`.
5. Return to Monotron and show the connected username.

Say:

> The same workspace-scoped flow is used for Instagram. Monotron reads only the
> connected account identity and messages.

### 3:00–3:40 — Instagram DM and reply

Show:

1. From another Instagram account, send a DM to the business account.
2. Show the conversation in the Monotron inbox.
3. Reply from the inbox.
4. Show the reply arriving in Instagram.

### 3:40–4:00 — Workspace isolation

Show:

- Disconnect a channel.
- Show that the connection is removed and the card returns to **Not connected**.

Say:

> Each channel connection belongs to one workspace. Disconnecting removes the stored
> connection and stops new messages from being imported for that channel.

## Common rejection reasons

- **Screencast does not show the permission being used.** Show the Facebook login,
  the permission grant, the customer message, and the reply in one video.
- **No visible customer message or reply.** Use a real phone number, not a `+1 555`
  sandbox number, for the WhatsApp portion.
- **Reviewer cannot log in.** Create the reviewer workspace and test the credentials
  before submitting.
- **Privacy policy or data deletion URL is missing.** Use
  `https://www.monotron.in/policies` for both.
- **Business verification is incomplete.** Finish it before submitting for Advanced
  Access.
